# HEIC decoder

`heic-to-1.6.5.js` is the unmodified CSP ES module build of heic-to 1.6.5, distributed under LGPL-3.0. It includes libheif 1.23.5.

Package and corresponding source: https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to

Package archive: https://registry.npmjs.org/heic-to/-/heic-to-1.6.5.tgz

Libheif source: https://github.com/strukturag/libheif/tree/v1.23.5

License: `heic-to-LICENSE.txt`. The decoder is a separate module and may be replaced with a compatible build. It is loaded only for HEIC/HEIF photos that cannot be decoded by the browser.
